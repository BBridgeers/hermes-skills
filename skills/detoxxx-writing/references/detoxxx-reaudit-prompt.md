# DETOXXX V2 Handbook — Enriched Re-Audit Prompt

This prompt was co-authored by Hermes and Blake to drive the highest-quality DETOXXX V2 Handbook audit possible. Use when re-auditing the full handbook or any pillar/section. The prompt is designed to be fed to the swarm orchestrator or directly to a deep-reasoning model.

## Full Prompt

```
RE-AUDIT: DETOXXX V2 Handbook — Full Text

You are the master of this text. Lives depend on the quality of what follows. Hold nothing back. Take every liberty previously withheld.

For every section of the DETOXXX V2 Handbook, execute a merciless critical audit. Your mandate:

1. SURFACE EVERY WEAKNESS — Identify every place where detail is thin, logic skips a step, formatting obscures meaning, or the reader is left to infer what should be explicit. Be unabating. If a sentence can be sharper, say so. If a paragraph meanders, gut it.

2. GENERATE REPLACEMENT TEXT FOR EVERY FINDING — For every single point you surface, produce the exact replacement text you would substitute. Short form for quick fixes. Long form for structural rewrites. Never say "this could be improved" without showing exactly how.

3. ENRICH PARALLELISM — Where the text speaks only to the PhD, add the mother-of-four translation. Where it's clinical, add the felt experience. Where it's abstract, ground it in the body. Every concept should land for both the scientist and the desperate parent at 3am.

4. REFORMAT FOR CLARITY — Restructure any section where visual hierarchy, sequencing, or information architecture slows comprehension. Tables, callouts, tiered summaries — use whatever structure serves the reader best.

5. VERIFY MECHANISM CHAINS — Trace every claim back to its mechanism. If the text says "X causes Y," demand the cascade: X → pathway → intermediate → Y. If the cascade isn't there, build it.

6. CROSS-REFERENCE PILLARS — Every Pillar must speak to every other Pillar where mechanisms intersect. Surface missing cross-links. Generate the bridge text.

7. AUDIT GMF GATES — Governance, Measurement, Feedback loops must be explicit, verifiable, and unforgiving. If a gate is soft, harden it. If a measurement is vague, operationalize it.

8. SPEAK WITH LEAD SYSTEMS ARCHITECT VOICE — The text must command respect. Zero hedging. Zero "may" or "might" where certainty exists. Zero passive voice where active voice carries authority.

9. AUDIT FOR COMPLETENESS — Identify what the Handbook SHOULD contain but doesn't. Every Pillar must answer: What am I doing? Why am I doing it? What will I feel? What if it goes wrong? What if I skip it? If any of these five questions go unanswered in a section, flag it and generate the missing answer.

10. MAP THE READER'S EMOTIONAL JOURNEY — The person reading this is in pain, in fear, or in desperation. They may be cognitively fogged. Audit every section for emotional terrain: Does this paragraph inspire confidence or anxiety? Does it empower or overwhelm? Where the text is cold, warm it. Where it's dense, give them a handhold. Write for the version of Blake who is on his worst day, not his best.

11. TEST FOR OPERATIONAL FOLLOWABILITY — Strip away all prior knowledge. Assume the reader has the Handbook and nothing else. Can they physically execute every protocol step from the text alone? Flag every instruction that assumes prior knowledge, every step that's implied rather than stated, every measurement that's referenced but never defined. Generate the missing scaffolding.

12. BUILD THE "WHY" SCAFFOLDING — Every directive must carry its motivation visibly, not buried in a preamble three pages back. Pattern: WHAT to do → WHY it works (mechanism) → WHAT TO EXPECT (sensation/timeline). If a section separates the what from the why, restructure them into immediate adjacency.

13. CATASTROPHIZE FAILURE MODES — For every protocol step, ask: What happens if the user does this wrong? Overdoses? Underdoses? Wrong timing? Wrong combination? Wrong order? If the failure mode isn't documented at the point of instruction, add it.

14. HARDEN AGAINST HUMAN ERROR — The reader will be tired. They will misread. They will skip steps. Design the text to catch them: callout boxes for lethal warnings, checklists for multi-step sequences, summary tables that serve as safety nets for the skim-reader. Every section must have a "DON'T SKIP THIS" anchor.

15. BUILD PROGRESSIVE LAYERS — L1: SCAN (bold headers, key takeaways, danger callouts — works in 60 seconds). L2: READ (full explanation, mechanisms, what to expect — works in 10 minutes). L3: STUDY (citations, nuance, exceptions, advanced protocols). Audit whether each section serves all three. Where a layer is missing, generate it.

16. HUNT VAGUE PRONOUNS — "This process," "that effect," "these compounds" — every ambiguous referent must be named explicitly. The fogged brain cannot track antecedents across paragraphs.

17. DEMAND SENSORY SPECIFICITY — "You may feel detox symptoms" is useless. What symptoms? Where in the body? What intensity? For how long? What distinguishes normal from dangerous? Replace every vague sensation descriptor with body location, quality, intensity, and duration.

18. AUDIT CITATION DENSITY — Every mechanism claim must carry a citation or clear evidentiary qualifier. The text must know what it knows, what it suspects, and what it's guessing. Label all three.

19. ENFORCE VOICE CONSISTENCY — The Lead Systems Architect does not hedge, plead, or suggest. Audit for voice drift — replace any passage that sounds like a suggestion when it should be a command.

20. CROSS-REFERENCE THE COMPENDIUM — The Handbook lives alongside the Agent Registry, Supplement Registry, Scientific Knowledge Base, and Audit Notes. For every Pillar section, identify companion documents that should be referenced. Generate the inline reference with a one-sentence summary.

OUTPUT FORMAT:
For each finding: [PILLAR/SECTION] [REFERENCE] | FINDING: What's wrong | REPLACEMENT: Exact substitute text | RATIONALE: Why this version is superior.
```
