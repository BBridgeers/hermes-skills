# Make/Model-Specific Fatal Flaws — Quick Reference

For vision-based condition assessment. These are industry-documented catastrophic pattern failures.
When the vision model identifies a make/model, it cross-references against this list and flags matches
with severity + estimated repair cost.

## NISSAN (pre-2020)
- **CVT transmission**: judder, hesitation, whining noise → $3-5K replacement. Affects: Altima, Sentra, Rogue, Versa, Pathfinder
- **VQ timing chain rattle**: VQ35DE/VQ40DE engines (Pathfinder, Maxima, Frontier, Xterra)
- **Radiator cross-contamination**: Xterra/Frontier 2005-2010 — coolant mixes with ATF via radiator failure
- **Catalytic converter failure**: common on 4-cylinder models

## FORD
- **PowerShift DCT**: Focus/Fiesta 2012-2016 — shuddering, clutch slip, TCM failure → AVOID ENTIRELY
- **5.4L Triton V8**: F-150 2004-2010 — cam phaser rattle ($2K), spark plug ejection, timing chain
- **EcoBoost 3.5L/3.7L**: timing chain stretch, carbon buildup on intake valves, turbo failure
- **6.0L PowerStroke**: EGR cooler, head gasket, oil cooler failures → $5-10K bulletproofing required
- **6.4L PowerStroke**: cracked pistons, fuel dilution → AVOID COMPLETELY
- **Fusion/MKZ**: electronic power steering rack failure

## TOYOTA
- **2AZ-FE 2.4L**: Camry/Corolla 2007-2011 — excessive oil consumption (piston ring defect). Check for blue exhaust smoke
- **Tacoma frame rust**: 2005-2010 — factory buyback program. Check entire frame, especially rear section
- **3.0L V6 sludge**: early 2000s Camry/ES300 — from infrequent oil changes. Check under oil cap
- **Prius hybrid battery**: degradation after 10+ years ($2-4K). Inverter pump failure
- **VVT-i rattle**: 2AR-FE engine — cold start metallic rattle for 1-2 seconds

## HONDA
- **V6 automatic transmission**: Accord/Odyssey 2000-2006 — catastrophic failure common. Check for delayed engagement, harsh shifts
- **1.5T oil dilution**: Civic/CR-V 2016-2018 — fuel in oil, especially cold climates. Smell dipstick for fuel
- **4-cylinder oil consumption**: Accord 2008-2012 — excessive consumption
- **Civic cracked block**: 2006-2009 — factory defect, coolant leak from block
- **CR-V AC compressor**: 2017-2019 — premature failure

## HYUNDAI / KIA
- **Theta II 2.0T/2.4L**: 2011-2019 — rod bearing failure, engine seizure. RECALL/CLASS ACTION. Listen for knocking at 2-3K RPM
- **Nu 1.8L/2.0L**: similar rod bearing issues affecting Elantra, Forte, Soul
- **Elantra piston slap**: 2017-2019 — excessive oil consumption
- **Sorento engine fire**: 2011-2018 — fire risk, recall issued
- **Steering coupler**: Sonata/Optima — clicking sound when turning (cheap fix, $15 part)

## CHEVROLET / GM
- **AFM/DFM lifter collapse**: 5.3L/6.2L V8 (Silverado/Tahoe/Suburban 2007+) — bent pushrods, cam damage → $4-7K
- **3.6L V6 timing chain**: Traverse/Acadia/Enclave 2007-2017 — chain stretch, rattle on startup
- **Equinox/Terrain 2.4L**: excessive oil consumption, timing chain failure
- **Cruze 1.4L Turbo**: coolant leaks (water outlet, thermostat), PCV failure in valve cover
- **Malibu EPS failure**: 2013-2015 — electronic power steering failure (recall)
- **Cobalt/G5 ignition**: recall — engine can shut off while driving (fatal defect, should be fixed by now)

## BMW
- **N63/S63 V8**: valve stem seal failure → blue smoke on startup ($5-8K). Coolant lines in valley pan leak
- **N54 twin-turbo**: wastegate rattle, HPFP failure, injector failure, carbon buildup
- **N20 4-cylinder**: timing chain guide failure pre-2015 (engine-destroying if chain jumps)
- **E90 electric water pump**: sudden failure → instant overheat, stranding risk
- **Cooling system plastics**: expansion tank, radiator neck, water pump — ALL plastic, ALL crack with age
- **Valve cover gasket**: leaks onto hot exhaust manifold → FIRE RISK

## SUBARU
- **EJ25 head gasket**: pre-2010 2.5L engines — external coolant/oil leak ($2-3K). Check for oil on exhaust manifold
- **FA/FB oil consumption**: 2011-2015 — excessive consumption, piston ring issue
- **CVT valve body**: 2010-2018 — solenoid failure causing harsh engagement
- **Wheel bearings**: common failure before 100K across all models

## CHRYSLER / DODGE / JEEP
- **TIPM**: Totally Integrated Power Module failure — fuel pump relay, random electrical gremlins. Affects nearly all 2007-2016 models
- **Pentastar 3.6L cylinder head**: 2011-2013 — left bank failure (misfire, ticking)
- **Grand Cherokee shifter**: 2014-2016 — electronic shifter rollaway risk (recall)
- **Ram exhaust manifold bolts**: 2009-2018 — bolts break, exhaust leak, expensive extraction
- **Ram lifter failure**: MDS-equipped 5.7L Hemi — lifter collapse, cam damage
- **Jeep death wobble**: Wrangler — front suspension oscillation at highway speed

## VOLKSWAGEN / AUDI
- **2.0T TSI timing chain**: pre-2013 — tensioner failure → engine destruction. Check for rattle on cold start
- **DSG mechatronic**: $2-3K failure — harsh shifting, stuck in gear
- **Water pump/thermostat**: leaks across ALL models — check coolant level
- **2.0T oil consumption**: piston ring defect → 1qt every 1000 miles
- **Coil pack failure**: premature, across all TSI engines

## MAZDA
- **RX-8 rotary**: apex seal failure → rebuild every 60-80K miles ($4-6K). Check hot start compression
- **Mazda6 rust**: 2003-2008 — rear wheel wells and trunk perforation
- **CX-7 Turbo**: turbocharger failure, VVT actuator, timing chain
- **Mazda3 shock mounts**: 2004-2009 — rear shock mount corrosion

## MERCEDES-BENZ
- **SBC brake**: 2003-2006 E/S/CLS class — pump failure (was under extended warranty)
- **Airmatic suspension**: struts ($1K each) and compressor failure
- **M272 balance shaft**: 2004-2008 V6 — requires engine-out repair
- **722.6/722.9 transmission**: conductor plate failure → stuck in gear, limp mode
