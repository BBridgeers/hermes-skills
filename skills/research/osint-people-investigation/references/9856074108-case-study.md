## Lessons Learned
- **Tavily was the workhorse:** Consistently returned useful results when Serper, Brave, and Exa either failed or returned noise.
- **Firecrawl credits are a recurring bottleneck:** Had to route all web_extract calls through web_extract_plus with linkup/tavily.
- **BBB is the single best source** for business owner identity, entity type, incorporation date, and alternate contacts.
- **Area code mismatch is a signal:** Louisiana area code for a Colorado business hinted at prior geographic history (confirmed by State Farm employment timeline).
- **Aggregator cross-listing is common:** Spotlight Tile showed up in initial searches for the number but was a stale association — always cross-check the primary listing's own contact page.
