# Lead Qualification & Scoring Framework
**Last Updated:** June 12, 2026 — refined after user correction

## Core Principle
Heat-map ALL enriched leads AFTER enrichment completes. NEVER pre-select a target from stale data. Data decides the top slot — not inertia. Score every ghost, rank, then build specs for top 3-5.

## Scoring Weights

### Primary Qualifiers (Must-Have Data Points)

| Metric | Weight | Rationale |
|--------|--------|-----------|
| **Vertical "Chances at Bat"** | ×2.5 | NOT ticket price — average jobs/tickets per month for that industry. Plumbers get 5-10 emergency calls/week. Tree services get 2-3/week. Pest control gets 15-20/week. More frequent customer touchpoints = more lost revenue without a website = more urgency. |
| **Emergency-Service Vertical** | ×2.0 | Plumbing, HVAC, electrical, garage door, roofing (leak emergencies), locksmith. These businesses lose money by the hour without proper digital presence. |
| **Owner Name Confidence** | ×1.5 | High-confidence owner name enables personalized outreach. "Hey [Name], I noticed..." converts 3-5x better than "To whom it may concern." |
| **City Affluence** | ×1.3 | Southlake, Colleyville, Westlake, University Park, Highland Park — higher disposable income, higher willingness to pay $1,500-5,500. |

### Bonus Signals (Additive, Never Gates)

| Metric | Points | Rationale |
|--------|--------|-----------|
| **Any GBP Presence** | +5 | Photos, hours, posts, or reviews = confirmed real business. These are digital laggards — ANY GBP activity is signal, NOT a qualification gate. Count it, don't gate on it. |
| **5+ Reviews** | +8 | Lower the bar. These owners don't understand digital. Getting ANY customer to leave a review is hard. 5+ reviews = they're actively serving real customers. 15+ is an unreasonably high bar for this demographic. |
| **DBA Filed at County Level** | +10 | Shows they at least did SOMETHING legal — they have a business identity. Strong signal of legitimacy. |
| **Phone Number Verified** | +15 | Can you actually reach them? If the phone is disconnected, the lead is dead regardless of all other scores. |

### Anti-Qualifiers (Red Flags)

| Red Flag | Score Impact | Rationale |
|----------|-------------|-----------|
| **Phone disconnected/wrong number** | -100 (instant disqualify) | Can't close what you can't contact |
| **Business permanently closed** | -100 (instant disqualify) | Self-explanatory |
| **Low-ticket vertical with low frequency** | -20 | E.g., wedding photographer (1 job/customer, 1-2/month). Low urgency for a website. |

## User Corrections Embedded (June 2026)

1. **Ticket size is the wrong metric.** "$500+ per job" eliminates too many viable industries. The correct measure is "number of customer touchpoints per month" — how many chances do they have to lose business without a website? A $100 pest control job that happens 20 times/week has more lost-revenue pressure than a $2,000 tree removal that happens twice a month.

2. **GBP activity is a BONUS, not a gate.** These business owners are digital laggards in 2026. They don't know what a Google Business Profile is. If there's anything there at all — photos, hours, a single post — it's a hot signal that the business is real and someone answers the phone. Don't gate on review count or profile completeness.

3. **15+ reviews is an unreasonably high bar** for people who don't understand digital. Getting a human to leave a Google review is genuinely difficult. 5+ reviews = they're serving customers. That's enough.
