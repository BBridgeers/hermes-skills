## Option 1: We Just Update Your DNS (Simplest — No Transfer Needed)

If you want to **keep your domain where it is** and just point it to your new website, here's all you need to do:

1. **Log into your domain registrar** (GoDaddy, Namecheap, etc.)
2. Find **DNS Settings** or **Nameservers** for your domain
3. We'll give you two Cloudflare nameserver addresses that look like this:
   - `aria.ns.cloudflare.com`
   - `ben.ns.cloudflare.com`
4. Replace your current nameservers with the two we provide
5. Save and you're done

**That's it.** Your website will be live within 24–48 hours (usually much faster — sometimes minutes).

## Option 2: Transfer Your Domain to Cloudflare (For Full Ownership Consolidation)

If you'd like to have everything — your domain AND your website hosting — managed in one place (Cloudflare), we can fully transfer your domain over. Cloudflare sells domains at cost (no markup), so it's often cheaper than renewing at GoDaddy.

### What You'll Need to Do

**Step 1 — Unlock your domain**

Log into wherever your domain is registered and look for a setting called **Domain Lock**, **Transfer Lock**, or **Registrar Lock**. Turn it off. This is a security feature that prevents unauthorized transfers — you're turning it off temporarily so we can move it.

- In GoDaddy: My Products → Domains → click your domain → Settings → scroll to "Domain Lock" → toggle off
- In Namecheap: Domain List → click Manage → find "Transfer Lock" → set to OFF

**Step 2 — Get Your Auth Code**

This is like a temporary password that authorizes the transfer. It's also called an EPP code or Authorization Code.

- In GoDaddy: Once you unlock the domain, GoDaddy usually emails it to you automatically
- In Namecheap: Domain List → Manage → Auth Code → click "Send by Email"

Forward that code to us as soon as you get it. It expires in 7–30 days depending on your registrar.

**Step 3 — Approve the Transfer**

Once we initiate the transfer, Cloudflare will send an email to the address on file for your domain. You'll need to click **Approve Transfer** in that email. Check your spam folder if you don't see it within an hour.

**Step 4 — Wait**

Transfers take **2–7 business days** to complete. We'll let you know the moment it's done. Your website stays live the entire time — there's no downtime.
